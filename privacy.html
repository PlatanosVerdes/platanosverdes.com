PlatanosVerdes

Privacy policy

Last updated 27 October 2026

PlatanosVerdes runs a few self-hosted services under platanosverdes.com: private ones for a small group of family and friends, and Apex, a motorcycle route planner anyone can use. This page explains what each of them keeps when you sign in, and why.

Private services

Sign-in goes through Cloudflare Access. When you choose Google, Google shares your email address, your name and your profile picture with it; when you choose a login code, you give your email address. Nothing else from your Google account is requested.

Your email address is only used to decide whether you may open a service, and to show who is signed in on that service's own page.

  • Cloudflare keeps a record of each sign-in (email address, time, IP address, service) as part of Cloudflare Access. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • The lists of who may open each service hold email addresses only.
  • A service may keep who invited you and when, so its owner knows how you got access.
  • Your browser keeps a session cookie so you do not have to sign in on every visit.

Apex

Apex can be used without an account. Signing in with Google is only needed to save routes and link Google Maps lists; Google then shares your email address, your name and your profile picture. Nothing else from your Google account is requested.

  • Apex keeps, on its own server, your email address, name and picture, the routes you save (up to 200) and the Google Maps lists you link (up to 10), with the places in them.
  • Your browser keeps a session cookie for 30 days, and the route you are drawing so it is still there when you come back.
  • To draw a route, Apex sends its points, never who you are, to the public Valhalla routing server of FOSSGIS; to search a place it sends what you type to Photon (Komoot), and the points of new stops to Nominatim (OpenStreetMap) to name them.
  • A linked Google Maps list is read again from Google every few hours, through its share link, so it stays up to date.
  • The map itself is loaded by your browser from OpenFreeMap and, for the satellite view, from Esri, who see your IP address as any map site would.
  • Apex is reached through Cloudflare, which sees your IP address as it passes requests on. Apex holds it in memory for a short while to stop anyone asking too often, and does not store it.

What it is not used for

None of this is used for advertising, sold, or shared with anyone beyond what is listed above.

Removing your data

Write to platanosverdes@googlegroups.com, the support address shown on the Google sign-in screen, or ask the person who invited you, and your address will be taken off every list and your Apex account, routes and lists deleted. Cloudflare's own sign-in records expire on Cloudflare's schedule.
